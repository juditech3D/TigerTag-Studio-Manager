> ⚠️ **Unofficial fork.** This is a personal fork of Tiger Studio Manager. It is neither published, maintained nor supported by the TigerTag Project, so please do not contact them about it.

Label printers, a scanner switch and a gentler TigerScale, on top of 2.59.2-fork.2.

## Added

- **Print labels without the print dialog.** The label card gains a **Printer** list: a **Marklife P15** over Bluetooth (Connect, then pick it), a **Brother QL** on the network (the roll is read from the printer, and sizes it cannot take are greyed out), or any printer installed on the computer. Each printer remembers its own label format, and its state (ready, in error) shows while the card is open. Setup and troubleshooting: `docs/LABEL-PRINTERS.md`.
- **Scanner switch.** A button next to *Select* sets what a USB barcode scanner does: open the product from anywhere (default), only from the search bar, or nothing.
- **TigerScale: Wake up.** A button on the scale's card lights its screen without pressing anything on it. The live view and the screenshot wake a sleeping scale the same way.

## Changed

- **Bambu Lab spools need both chips.** A Bambu Lab spool is saved or linked only once both of its chips are read; until then Studio says which one is missing and how to place the spool.

## Fixed

- **Check for updates** shows one short sentence instead of the updater's raw error, and says to wait a few minutes when a release is still being published.
- The TigerScale live view's **Screenshot** button opens its preview in front of the live view instead of behind it.

## Known limitations

- **Windows and Linux only.** There is no macOS build in this release.
- **The Windows installer is not signed.** Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch: *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **Sign in with your e-mail and password.** Google sign-in needs the TigerTag Project's own OAuth credentials and does not work in this build.
- **Mounting a spool in a printer slot** (AMS, CFS, ACE…) is not working yet.
- Updates come from this fork's releases, not from the official ones.
