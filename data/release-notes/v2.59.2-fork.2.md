> ⚠️ **Unofficial fork.** This is a personal fork of Tiger Studio Manager. It is neither published, maintained nor supported by the TigerTag Project, so please do not contact them about it.

A small update on top of 2.59.2-fork.1, also used to check that updates arrive from this fork.

## Added

- **Request links.** A `tigertag://report?…` link opens Studio's request window (Improvements & suggestions) with its type, product, title and text already filled in. You still add your screenshots, tick that the request is public and press Send yourself: a link never sends anything on its own.

## Known limitations

- **Windows and Linux only.** There is no macOS build: it needs the TigerTag Project's Apple certificate.
- **The Windows installer is not signed.** Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch: *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **Sign in with your e-mail and password.** Google sign-in needs the TigerTag Project's own OAuth credentials and does not work in this build.
- **Mounting a spool in a printer slot** (AMS, CFS, ACE…) is not working yet.
- Updates come from this fork's releases, not from the official ones.
