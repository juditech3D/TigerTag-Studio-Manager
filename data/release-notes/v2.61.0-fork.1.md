The official Tiger Studio 2.61.0, with this fork's additions carried over on top of it.

## New from the official 2.61.0

- **Printer cameras no longer flicker.** At launch, and each time a printer was connected or disconnected, every camera on the Printers board went off and came back. The board is now redrawn only when it really changes.
- **Requests: search and filters.** "All requests" and "My requests" get a search field and Status / Repository / Type filters, and each request shows where it was sent.
- **Live screen screenshot.** On a Tiger Scale or Tiger Touch, the screenshot window now opens in front of the live screen.
- **Search bar:** its lists no longer show above a side card opened over it.
- **Catalogue:** 24,947 products.

## Added in this fork

- **Detached camera wall.** While the wall is open in its own window, the main window shows a notice with a "Bring the wall back" key instead of a second copy of every feed. In the detached window, each card can stop or restart its camera, and the title bar has "Stop all cameras" / "Show all cameras".
- **Printers board:** the Arrange / zoom / "Stop all cameras" bar has its own row above the plan, so it no longer covers the top of a card.
- **A macOS version** (Apple chip and Intel), tested by Mac users from the community. The app asks macOS for local network access, so it can reach your TigerScale, TigerPOD and printers.
- **"Something wrong?" bubble** at the bottom right: describe a problem, add a capture of the window if you want, and it is sent straight to the fork's Discord with the app version and the system. No e-mail, password or stock is sent.

## Known limitations

- **The Mac app is not signed by Apple.** macOS blocks it the first time: see *Opening the app on macOS* below. It does not update itself: download each new `.dmg` by hand.
- **The Windows installer is not signed.** Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch: *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **Sign in with your e-mail and password.** Google sign-in needs the TigerTag Project's own OAuth credentials and does not work in this build.
- **Mounting a spool in a printer slot** (AMS, CFS, ACE…) is not working yet.
- Updates come from this fork's releases, not from the official ones.

## Opening the app on macOS / Ouvrir l'application sur macOS

**English.** Drag the app into Applications and open it once, click **OK** (not *Move to Bin*), then allow it **once**:
- **macOS 15 Sequoia or later**: *System Settings → Privacy & Security*, *Security* section → **Open Anyway**, then your password. If the button is missing, try opening the app again, then come back.
- **macOS 14 Sonoma or earlier**: **right-click** the app → **Open** → **Open**.
- **If that does not work, or macOS says the app "is damaged"**: open *Terminal* (Applications → Utilities) and run `xattr -cr "/Applications/Tiger Studio Manager.app"`, then open the app.
- When macOS asks for **local network** access, click **Allow**. To turn it back on: *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network*.

**Français.** Glissez l'application dans Applications et ouvrez-la une fois, cliquez sur **OK** (pas sur *Placer dans la corbeille*), puis autorisez-la **une fois** :
- **macOS 15 Sequoia et plus récent** : *Réglages Système → Confidentialité et sécurité*, rubrique *Sécurité* → **Ouvrir quand même**, puis votre mot de passe. Si le bouton n'apparaît pas, réessayez d'ouvrir l'application, puis revenez.
- **macOS 14 Sonoma et plus ancien** : **clic droit** sur l'application → **Ouvrir** → **Ouvrir**.
- **Si ça ne marche pas, ou si macOS dit que l'application « est endommagée »** : ouvrez *Terminal* (Applications → Utilitaires) et lancez `xattr -cr "/Applications/Tiger Studio Manager.app"`, puis ouvrez l'application.
- Quand macOS demande l'accès au **réseau local**, cliquez sur **Autoriser**. Pour le réactiver : *Réglages Système → Confidentialité et sécurité → Réseau local*.
