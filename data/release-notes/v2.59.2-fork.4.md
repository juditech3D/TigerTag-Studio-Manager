Labels on A4, a smoother mouse wheel and a few handy keys, on top of 2.59.2-fork.3.

## Added

- **Labels on an A4 sheet.** For the print dialog and installed printers, pick a **Sheet**: the label's own size, A4 with the labels from the top-left corner, or A4 on a centred grid. The preview shows the whole sheet. Set the number of **Copies** (or press **Stock (n)** to print one per spool of that product in stock), the spacing between labels and the margin of the sheet. Further sheets are added when one is full, and − / + zooms the preview.
- **Labels for several spools at once.** Tick spools in the inventory, then press **Labels** in the selection bar: one label per spool, printed in one run, with a single PDF holding them all. The card follows the selection while it is open.
- **Choose a label's values on the label itself.** A large drawing of the label, in the shape of the chosen format, shows each values line where it prints: click it to choose what it shows.
- **Show all / Stop all cameras** keys on the Printers board.
- **Select All** in the tile views: in select mode, it ticks every spool the filter shows.

## Changed

- **Mouse wheel on the Storage, Printers and camera wall plans.** The wheel now scrolls the plan (sideways when the plan is wide but not tall), and Ctrl + wheel (⌘ on a Mac) zooms it. Ctrl + / Ctrl − press the plan's zoom keys instead of zooming the whole window. The scrollbars are thin again.

## Known limitations

- **Windows and Linux only.** There is no macOS build in this release.
- **The Windows installer is not signed.** Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch: *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **Sign in with your e-mail and password.** Google sign-in needs the TigerTag Project's own OAuth credentials and does not work in this build.
- **Mounting a spool in a printer slot** (AMS, CFS, ACE…) is not working yet.
- Updates come from this fork's releases, not from the official ones.
