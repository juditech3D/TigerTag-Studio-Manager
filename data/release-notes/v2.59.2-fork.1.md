> ⚠️ **Unofficial fork.** This is a personal fork of Tiger Studio Manager. It is neither published, maintained nor supported by the TigerTag Project, so please do not contact them about it.

First release of the fork, built on the official Tiger Studio Manager 2.59.2.

## Added

- **A TigerScale as a TigerPOD.** While the TigerPOD tool is open on a scale running the [alternative TigerScale firmware](https://github.com/juditech3D/Tiger-Scale-V3), its two readers show up in Studio as two more RFID readers. Every chip feature works on them as on a USB reader: open the spool of a chip put down, write a blank chip, TigerTag+ upgrade, restore, erase, NFC tools.
- **Bambu Lab chips.** A Bambu Lab chip on a USB reader (ACR122U) or on the scale is read: material, colour, weight, diameter and temperatures, and the catalogue opens on the Bambu Lab product of the closest colour. Read only, nothing is written to the chip.
- **Barcode and QR scanner anywhere.** A USB scanner works anywhere in the app: the scanned code opens the matching catalogue product, ready to add as a spool, and a spool QR link opens that spool. A scanner set to the US (QWERTY) layout also works on an AZERTY computer.

## Fixed

- **`.ttag` import in Import mode** works again.
- **A Feitian ROCKEY-ARM licence dongle** is no longer listed as an RFID reader.

## Known limitations

- **Windows and Linux only.** There is no macOS build: it needs the TigerTag Project's Apple certificate.
- **The Windows installer is not signed.** Windows SmartScreen warns on first launch: *More info* → *Run anyway*.
- **Sign in with your e-mail and password.** Google sign-in needs the TigerTag Project's own OAuth credentials and does not work in this build.
- **Mounting a spool in a printer slot** (AMS, CFS, ACE…) is not working yet.
- Updates come from this fork's releases, not from the official ones.
